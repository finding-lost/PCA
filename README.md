# Public Complaint Authority

Petty grievances handled seriously.

---

## Mission

The Public Complaint Authority was established to address concerns overlooked by others, dismissed by common sense, or simply too important to ignore for reasons we have yet to determine.

---

## Purpose

The Public Complaint Authority is a satirical website dedicated to acknowledging the everyday frustrations of modern life with an unnecessary level of professionalism.

While the Authority possesses no actual governmental powers, it remains committed to reviewing grievances in the spirit of public service, bureaucracy, and good humor.

---

## Design Philosophy

The website should appear authentic before revealing its satirical nature.

Humor comes from presentation rather than exaggeration.

---

## Tone

- Deadpan
- Bureaucratic
- Respectful
- Dry
- Never mean-spirited

---

## Motto

Petty grievances handled seriously.
