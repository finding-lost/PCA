Public Complaint Authority Disclaimer

The Public Complaint Authority is not a government agency, governmental entity, regulatory authority, legal authority, or any other organization with actual authority to resolve, investigate, enforce, or otherwise address complaints.

Submission of a complaint does not create an obligation for the Public Complaint Authority to take action, provide a response, contact another party, or resolve the matter in question.

The Public Complaint Authority may, at its discretion, edit, publish, share, respond to, or otherwise make use of submitted complaints and supporting information for entertainment, promotional, or other lawful purposes.

By submitting a complaint, you acknowledge that the Public Complaint Authority is operated for entertainment purposes and that any response, commentary, sarcasm, or other consideration your complaint receives should not be interpreted as professional, legal, governmental, or administrative assistance.

Please do not submit confidential, highly sensitive, or personally identifying information that you would not want potentially shared publicly.

The Public Complaint Authority appreciates your understanding of its very limited authority.
